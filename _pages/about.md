I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Civil, Environmental and Ocean Engineering at Stevens Institute of Technology.

My research focuses on advancing quantitative precipitation estimation and forecasting by integrating ground-based observations, weather radar, and numerical weather prediction data to address observational gaps and reduce uncertainties.

As part of my doctoral research, I have developed and deployed [low-cost IoT-based rainfall and water level monitoring sensors](https://web.stevens.edu/ismart/land_products/NJFloodNet.html) in New Jersey, with ongoing efforts to expand the network statewide. I have also developed a precipitation nowcasting framework that combines MRMS observations with HRRR model predictions to improve short-term rainfall forecasts.

I also contributed to the deployment of a dual-polarization [X-band weather radar](https://www.stevens.edu/news/stevens-institute-of-technology-installs-advanced-x-band-weather-radar-to) at Stevens Institute of Technology as part of the [Stevens High-resolution Atmospheric Radar Project (SHARP)](https://web.stevens.edu/ismart/atmosphere_products/sharp.html). My current research focuses on evaluating its performance against the NEXRAD network.
