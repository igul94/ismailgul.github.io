### Authors

**Ismail Gul**, Mohamed Abdelkader, Kyle Mackelburg, and Marouane Temimi

### Abstract

This study presents a low-cost Internet of Things (IoT)-based sensor network designed for simultaneous rainfall and urban flood monitoring. The system integrates optical rainfall sensors and ultrasonic water-level sensors to provide high-temporal-resolution observations in flood-prone urban environments. The network was deployed in Hoboken and Westwood, New Jersey, as an initial step toward establishing NJFloodNet. The study evaluates sensor performance, temperature-related measurement uncertainties, and the relationship between rainfall and urban inundation.
