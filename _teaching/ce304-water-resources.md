I served as a Teaching Assistant for CE 304: Water Resources Engineering during the Spring 2025 and Spring 2026 semesters.

The course covers fundamental concepts in engineering hydrology, including precipitation analysis, rainfall–runoff relationships, hydrograph analysis, hydrologic and hydraulic routing, groundwater hydrology, and water resources planning and management.
